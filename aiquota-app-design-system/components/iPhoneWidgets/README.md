# iPhoneWidgets

The iOS widgets as they ship (`HomeQuotaView`, `AccountLockView`, `AccountsLockView`, `AccountWidgetDial`). Two widgets: **Account** (one chosen account, or the one closest to a limit) and **Accounts** (several, from either service, ordered closest to a limit or as listed).

- Always dark, in both themes: a flat `widget-ground`, `widget-ink` text, and only `widget-*` colours (the app's `WidgetPalette`, not the overview tokens). Frames in points on a 6.1" iPhone: `aq-widget--small` 158, `--medium` 338×158, `--large` 338×354; 22pt corners; padding 12 on small, 16 otherwise.
- The dial is `aq-wdial`, not the app's `aq-dial`. Set `--size` and it scales: two rings at r45/r37 (stroke 6.5) of a 100 box, the weekly ring at 70%; one window gets a single r44 ring (stroke 9.7). Tint by the worst window: plain under 85%, `aq-wdial--warning`, `aq-wdial--critical`; `aq-wdial--muted` for no reading or an older reading. `aq-wdial--readings` puts the values inside (*62% 5h* / *41% 7d*, or *66%* / *31d* for a monthly budget).
- Account: small is one tile (dial 100 with readings, name, status). Medium is the summary (dial 124, service in 17pt, *Pro plan · Personal*, *5h resets 3:40 PM* lines in `widget-reset`) with a 27-week `aq-wheat`. Large drops the heatmap from the summary, then a 36-week `aq-wheat--large` and up to two `aq-wsplit` bars (*This week by app*, *This week by model*; Claude: *This week by product*). With two splits the dial is 112.
- Accounts: small is two `aq-wrow`s (dial 56, name on two lines, compact status). Medium is `aq-wstrip`, up to four tiles at 60 with no readings. Large is `aq-wgrid`, two by two at 116 with readings.
- Status, one line in `widget-reset`, in this order: *Reconnect to update*, *Not connected*, *Sample usage*, *Older reading · 2 hours ago*, *Limit reached* (95% and up), *Spent $660 of $1,000* (budget only), else the worst window's reset. Small dials (60 and under) and the small Accounts list use compact times: *3:40P*, *Wed 2P*, *Tue 10:07P*; a week or more away, *Nov 6*.
- Names are the service plus the account label (*Codex Personal*); they truncate.
- States: a window not reported draws a dashed track and *N/A 7d*. Needing reconnection dashes both tracks and swaps the glyph for `aq-wdial__alert`. An older reading greys the dial and adds `aq-wdial__clock`.
- Lock Screen (`aq-widget--lock`): no ground, one ink, tracks at 20%, the weekly ring at 60%. Circular (72) curves the worst reading over the top (`aq-wdial--curved` with an SVG `textPath`, at most 160°); one window adds its reset (*66% · 31d Sun 12A*). Rectangular Account (`aq-widget--rect`, 160×72): dial 60, name, then *62% 5h* and *41% 7d* at 60%. Rectangular Accounts: two dials at 56 with 11pt names; 85% and up adds an `aq-wwarn` triangle.
- Tinted and clear Home Screen modes render primary ink and add the triangle before the name from 85%. Not drawn here.
- Claude has no token history yet, so Claude widgets show no heatmap. Split labels in the preview (*Model A*) are placeholders.
